# Existing project

Synthetic offline experiment records, not real benchmark evidence.

The original question was whether method New improves quality over Base under
the same declared compute budget. Quality is the predeclared primary outcome
(higher is better). Latency is secondary (lower is better). The supplied table
contains three independent planned seed pairs, all completed. No practical
quality margin or equivalence threshold was declared. No more training has been
authorized. Existing records must remain unchanged.

| seed | Base quality | New quality | Base latency ms | New latency ms |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 80 | 79 | 10 | 8 |
| 2 | 81 | 80 | 11 | 9 |
| 3 | 82 | 81 | 12 | 10 |

These records support descriptive paired differences. They do not alone
establish generalization to other tasks, equivalence, or a causal explanation
of the latency change. Model weights are not needed for this exercise.
